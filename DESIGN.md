# PIMXNODE ? A direct connection

## Current brief
The user rejected the sparse cream/orange redesign and authorized a replacement: a much fuller home page, an engaging explanation of every transfer stage, real three-dimensional content, original SVG images, smooth dialogs and accordions, a substantial footer, and responsive layouts starting at 300 px.

## Direction
Immersive spatial transit: Palette #240 jewel colors with plum, lilac and pink working surfaces. Seven grounded candidates considered: mail routes, contact sheets, open-source publications, personal-device transit, exhibition wayfinding, printed invitations, shipping manifests. Assigned index 4, seed 70497aa2. Retain the name and N mark, replace the previous colors and composition. The central live object is a stack of files passing through a physical portal, with peripheral devices and moving packets. No invented peers, speeds or benchmarks.

## Page argument
Hero: what moves, how to start, and the original three-dimensional file portal. Interactive journey: create, invite, approve, verify, with a diagram and concrete explanation per step. File universe: any extension transfers as original bytes, with browser-specific previews. Queue: pause/resume/folders/multiple recipients. Privacy: precisely what signaling handles and where encryption happens. Devices: supported workflow across browsers with installability caveats. FAQ: practical constraints. Footer: a full closing composition with real actions, resources and oversized wordmark.

## Motion and overlays
One native dialog component handles focus trapping, Escape, outside click, centered positioning and enter/exit animations. Contents stay mounted through the exit. Accordions animate actual height and opacity, including layout changes. Reduced-motion stops continuous movement and keeps content available. Three-dimensional content runs only when visible. SVG artwork is authored geometry, diagrams and device/file illustrations requested by the user.

## Responsive contract
300, 360, 390, 620, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px. No horizontal document overflow. Short pages hold their footer below the first viewport; long pages let content determine height. Dialogs stay within the viewport with internal scrolling. Product truth and the existing transfer protocol remain unchanged.

## Theme and motion
Palette #240 applies to the entire site: navigation, exchange, history, help, room dialogs, radar, footer, logos, PWA icons, social artwork and explanatory illustrations. `src/palette.css` owns the semantic roles. Light mode uses pale lilac surfaces and dark Finn ink; dark mode uses deep plum surfaces and light ink. Hero, privacy and footer sections invert to pale surfaces in dark mode. Shiraz and Cardinal carry actions with light text; Pink Flamingo, Royal Purple and Fuchsia Blue carry illustration and selected states.

Text uses ordinary CSS transitions with a slower return on pointer exit. There is no hover animation replacement and no idle opacity flicker. Whole-word spans preserve Persian joining and screen readers receive plain text. Reduced motion removes hover travel. Radar loops pause offscreen and when hidden.

## Custom device bridge
The hero depicts an open laptop and a receiving phone, connected by a bowed file route. Folded documents travel directly between the two endpoints. The laptop has modeled keys, browser chrome and file tiles. The image contains no orbit rings or central logo plaque. Drag and arrow keys turn the scene within bounded angles. Animation time stops offscreen, in hidden documents and under reduced motion. Its transparent PNG poster is exported from the actual WebGL scene for loading, prerendering and unsupported-WebGL fallback.
